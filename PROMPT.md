SCENE

On a snowy pass at dawn, a white hand brushes snow off a mossy stone lantern, and YUKI, a slim white ronin, walks down stone steps through a broken red gate above a sea of clouds. His red eye slit glows; her cyan eyes breathe vapour. Omens: Sumi's reflection in a puddle turns to the camera with white eyes for three frames while he walks on; a white crack flickers across Yuki's reflection in frozen ice. She leaps off the cliff into the clouds. Match cut on the foot: his foot lands on the mirror lake and one perfect ripple ring crosses the reflected sunset.
They stop sixty metres apart, side on, the sun exactly between them. His thumb pushes the guard, a sliver of steel flashes; she sinks low and draws two blades in reverse grip. Stillness. One leftover raindrop slides off his scarf, hits the lake, and the camera tilts down into the reflection: under their feet the reflected sky holds a colossal face of mirror shards. Its white eyes open. Both look down.
They charge, meet in two impact frames, pass each other and stab their blades into the mirror at the giant's eye. Silence. Cracks race to the horizon and the giant pushes up into the real sky, glass and water pouring off it. Its shard sword carves a wall of spray as they leap over it. Yuki runs up its arm and cracks its face; a red-gold aura ignites along Sumi's blade as his scarf burns into embers. They leap from opposite sides into the sun and cut a giant X through its chest. One second of nothing. The X splits the clouds for kilometres, and the giant bursts into shards that fall like snow. The lake heals. He sheathes with a click; they bow with the sun between them, and walk away in opposite directions, fading to gold.
